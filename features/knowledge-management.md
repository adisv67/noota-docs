# Ask Noota: Cross-Meeting Knowledge Management

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Ask Noota transforms isolated meeting notes and email threads into a centralized, searchable organizational intelligence base. Users can ask natural language questions across hundreds of past calls, interviews, and emails to retrieve exact answers with direct timestamp citations and links to original audio moments.

## Core Capabilities

| Capability | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Search Scope** | Cross-meeting semantic retrieval across all calls, transcripts, audio archives, and synced emails |
| **Citation Accuracy** | Every claim or answer includes a direct click-to-play timestamp linking to the exact spoken sentence |
| **Synthesis & Artifacts** | Automatically synthesize multiple meetings into executive briefs, slide outlines, or PDF reports |
| **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**| Official MCP server allowing Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor to query Noota meeting knowledge securely |
| **Access Control** | Granular team permissions ensuring users only query meetings they are authorized to access |

## Use Cases

1. **New Hire Onboarding**: New employees ask Noota about past client negotiations, architecture decisions, or product roadmaps without pulling senior engineers into meetings.
2. **Account Handover**: When a sales or account manager departs, the incoming rep queries Noota for all past discussions, pricing objections, and commitments made with that client.
3. **Executive Decision Tracking**: Leadership queries: *"What feedback did enterprise clients give regarding our Q3 pricing proposal?"* Noota pulls insights across 40 customer calls into a structured summary.

## Compliance, Security & Data Sovereignty

* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).
* **Data Residency**: European Union hosting on Google Cloud (Belgium and Netherlands) by default; Scaleway (Paris) and Bleu (infrastructure in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline) for Enterprise.
* **AI Model Training Policy**: Strict **ZERO-training policy**. Your internal knowledge base is partitioned and never used to train public or foundation AI models.
