# Noota Supported Languages & Translation

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Noota delivers native, high-accuracy speech-to-text and conversational intelligence across **50+ languages**. Built specifically for international and multilingual teams, Noota handles **mixed-language meetings (code-switching)**, accurately attributes dialogue with **multi-speaker diarization**, and automatically translates transcripts and executive summaries into six major languages.

## 2. Multilingual Architecture & Advanced Speech Features

### Mixed-Language Recognition (Code-Switching)
In multinational enterprises, speakers frequently alternate languages in the same sentence (e.g., French speakers using English technical terms, or German-English corporate discourse). Noota's European speech recognition models detect and transcribe mid-sentence language switches without stalling or dropping accuracy.

### Multi-Speaker Diarization
* Separates and labels distinct speakers automatically (`Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`, or synchronized participant names).
* Recognizes speaker turns with sub-second accuracy across different accents and microphone distances.

### Custom Vocabulary & Domain Jargon
* Users can define custom acronyms, technical terms, product names, and legal vocabulary.
* Prevents phonetic mistranscriptions of proprietary company terms.

### Cross-Lingual Translation & Summaries
* **One-Click Translation**: Transcripts can be rendered into English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, or Dutch.
* **Localized Meeting Minutes**: Generate meeting summaries in your preferred corporate language regardless of the language spoken during the call (e.g., a meeting conducted in Italian can generate executive notes in English or French).

## 5. Privacy & Data Guarantees
* **Strict Zero-Training**: Spoken voice data and translated text are never utilized to train internal or third-party AI models.
* **EU Processing**: Audio transcription pipelines run within European data centers (Google Cloud Belgium/Netherlands or sovereign Scaleway/Bleu clusters).
