# Noota Talent: AI Recruitment Agent Suite

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Noota Talent (Taalent) is a dedicated recruitment intelligence platform that automates the end-to-end talent pipeline: job intake parsing, candidate sourcing across 800M+ global profiles, automated multichannel outreach, AI voice screening in 50+ languages, interview scorecards, and native two-way synchronization with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Scores and ratings are decision support; the recruiter makes the hiring decision.

## The 6 Specialized Recruitment Agents

| Agent | Functionality |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Intake Agent** | Extracts job requirements, must-have skills, and salary bands from hiring manager briefing calls |
| **2. Sourcing Agent** | Identifies matching passive and active candidates across 800M+ profiles globally |
| **3. Outreach Agent** | Generates personalized multi-touch sequences across Email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp |
| **4. Screening Agent** | Conducts automated AI voice screening in 50+ languages with structured transcript evaluation. Candidates must be told they are speaking with an AI system |
| **5. Interview Agent** | Structures live interviews against competency criteria and proposes ratings for the interviewer to review |
| **6. ATS Agent** | Automatically pushes candidate notes, ratings, audio clips, and scorecards into your ATS |

## Structured Scoring and Bias
* Noota structures candidate evaluation around defined rubric criteria and direct transcript answers.
* Structured criteria support consistency but do not by themselves remove bias. Test scoring for bias, keep human review, and document the hiring decision.

## Compliance, Security & Data Sovereignty

* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).
* **HR Data & GDPR**: Appointed Data Protection Officer (DPO), a candidate recording notice, and strict European data residency (Belgium/Netherlands; Scaleway France for Enterprise).
* **EU AI Act**: Evaluative features in recruitment are high-risk under Annex III, point 4(a). Confirm the classification and documentation with Noota before enabling scoring. Sourcing candidate profiles from external sources also triggers GDPR Article 14 notice duties for the recruiting organization.
* **AI Model Training Policy**: Strict **ZERO-training policy**. Candidate resumes, interviews, and scores are never used to train external AI models.
