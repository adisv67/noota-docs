# Noota Email Automation

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Noota Email Automation connects directly to Gmail and Microsoft Outlook to bridge meetings with asynchronous email workflows. It labels incoming messages, drafts contextual replies based on past meeting discussions, and converts email commitments into tracked tasks. The user maintains full editorial control: drafts are reviewed before sending.

## Core Capabilities

| Capability | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mailbox Providers** | Google Workspace (Gmail) & Microsoft 365 (Outlook / Exchange) |
| **Meeting-to-Email Context** | Drafts follow-ups automatically using exact quotes and commitments from recent calls |
| **Task Extraction** | Identifies action items inside email threads and syncs them to Notion, Slack, and CRMs |
| **User Control** | Human-in-the-loop review; emails are never sent autonomously without confirmation |
| **Tone Matching** | Learns your communication style and nuances to draft natural, personalized responses |

## How It Works

1. **Connect Mailbox**: One-click OAuth connection to Gmail or Outlook with enterprise-grade token encryption.
2. **Context Synthesis**: When an email arrives regarding a client account, Noota references recent meeting notes, previous email threads, and CRM data.
3. **Draft Generation**: Prepares an accurate reply containing the exact metrics, dates, and promises agreed upon in prior meetings.
4. **Approve & Send**: Review the draft in Noota or inside your native email client with a single click.

## Compliance, Security & Data Sovereignty

* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).
* **Data Residency**: Processed in Belgium and the Netherlands on Google Cloud infrastructure. Enterprise accounts can choose sovereign French hosting on Scaleway (Paris) and Bleu (infrastructure in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline).
* **AI Model Training Policy**: Strict **ZERO-training policy**. Customer emails and attachments are never used to train internal or third-party AI models.
