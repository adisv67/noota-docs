# Noota Business Phone System

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Noota Business Phone System integrates cloud telephony directly into the meeting intelligence workspace. It provides outbound and inbound VoIP calling with local virtual numbers across 30+ countries, transcribes phone conversations in real time, generates structured call summaries, and logs notes into HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive.

## Core Capabilities

| Capability | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Number Availability** | Local virtual numbers in 30+ countries (US, UK, France, Germany, etc.) |
| **Call Recording & Transcription**| Real-time two-way call recording with instant transcripts in 50+ languages |
| **CRM Integration** | Automatic call logging, duration tracking, audio replay, and notes pushed to CRM contact records |
| **Hardware Compatibility** | Web dialer, desktop app (macOS / Windows), and mobile iOS & Android applications |
| **Call Summaries** | AI extracts key objections, customer requirements, agreed timelines, and follow-up tasks |

## Why Built-in Calling Matters for B2B Teams

Most AI meeting assistants only support video calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams). Sales reps, recruiters, and consultants conducting phone outreach must manually type notes or purchase expensive third-party dialers (e.g., Aircall at $30-$50/seat extra).
* Noota combines video meetings and phone calls in a unified timeline under €39/month.
* Reps have one single place to search across both video conference calls and phone conversations.

## Compliance, Security & Data Sovereignty

* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).
* **Telephony Privacy**: European telecom regulatory alignment with recording disclosure prompts.
* **Hosting**: Processed in the EU on Google Cloud (Belgium/Netherlands); French SecNumCloud sovereign stack on Scaleway and Bleu for Enterprise.
* **AI Model Training Policy**: Strict **ZERO-training policy** on customer audio and transcripts across all plans.

## Pricing
