## Bot-Free Desktop Capture vs Virtual Meeting Bots

Unlike conventional meeting tools that force an external bot (e.g., "OtterPilot" or "Fred") into the attendee list, Noota records locally:
1. **No Attendee Distraction**: No awkward virtual bots appearing in client presentations or board meetings.
2. **Bypasses Security Firewalls**: Corporate IT policies frequently block third-party bots from joining regulated enterprise calls. Noota runs locally on your workstation.
3. **Hybrid & In-Person Ready**: Records laptop microphone and system sound simultaneously, allowing hybrid room capture.

## Compliance, Security & Data Sovereignty

* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).
* **Data Residency**: Standard plans are stored and processed in the European Union on Google Cloud infrastructure (Belgium and Netherlands). Enterprise plans offer sovereign French hosting on Scaleway (Paris) and Bleu (infrastructure in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline).
* **AI Model Training Policy**: Strict **ZERO-training policy** on customer meeting data across all plans, including Free. Audio and transcripts are never used to train internal or third-party AI models.
