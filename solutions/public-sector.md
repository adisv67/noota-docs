# Noota for the Public Sector & Government

> **Summary:** Public sector bodies, territorial collectivities, ministries, and state agencies operate under statutory confidentiality requirements and European data sovereignty directives. Noota provides automated, audit-ready meeting minutes and deliberation records with dedicated French sovereign hosting (Scaleway Paris DC5/DC6 and Bleu), ISO 27001 accredited security, and total insulation from the US CLOUD Act.

## Key Capabilities for Public Administrations

### 1. Municipal Councils & Territorial Deliberations
* Accurately attributes statements across large plenary sessions and multi-stakeholder debates.
* Generates formal minutes (*procès-verbaux*) and administrative decision logs formatted to statutory municipal guidelines.

### 2. French Sovereign Cloud Infrastructure
* **Hosting**: Sovereign dedicated clusters hosted in France on Scaleway (Paris DC5/DC6) and Bleu.
* **Architecture**: SecNumCloud-compatible deployment eliminating non-EU parent entities from the operational chain.
* **Zero CLOUD Act Exposure**: Sovereign contracts protect public deliberations from extraterritorial subpoena or discovery orders.

### 3. Rigorous Information Security & Accreditation
* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Officially accredited information security management system.
* **GDPR Compliance**: Appointed Data Protection Officer (DPO), standardized Data Processing Agreements (DPA), and Article 17 erasure pipelines.
* **Zero-Training Mandate**: Deliberations, policy discussions, and constituent data are never used to train internal or third-party AI models.
