# Noota for Consulting, Legal & Service Companies

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Professional services firms operate under strict client NDAs, professional secrecy standards, and rigorous compliance guidelines. Noota delivers discreet, bot-free meeting intelligence that captures billable hours, produces audit-grade client deliverables, and states European data residency and no AI model training on customer data.

---

## Why Advisory & Legal Firms Choose Noota

### 1. Bot-Free Client Confidentiality
* Traditional virtual bots visible in meeting rooms raise immediate client concerns regarding surveillance and unauthorized recording.
* Noota captures meetings locally via native macOS and Windows applications, maintaining client trust and professional decorum.

### 2. Strict Zero-Training AI Architecture
* Legal and consulting firms handle sensitive M&A data, proprietary intellectual property, and privileged client communications.
* Noota states that client audio, transcripts, and summaries are **never used to train AI models** (internal or third-party) across all tiers.

### 3. European Data Sovereignty & GDPR Compliance
* **Standard Infrastructure**: Data hosted within the European Union (Belgium and Netherlands on Google Cloud Platform).
* **Sovereign Enterprise Option**: French sovereign hosting on Scaleway (Paris DC5/DC6) and Bleu (infrastructure in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline), fully isolated from US CLOUD Act jurisdiction.
* **ISO 27001 Certified**: Validated information security management system (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress).

### 4. Billable Hours & Action Item Documentation
* Automatically captures meeting duration, participant lists, and chronological timelines to support transparent client billing.
* Transforms complex advisory discussions into structured executive deliverables:
  * Formal meeting minutes and steering committee recaps.
  * Risk registers, open decisions, and accountability matrices.
  * Direct synchronization with Notion, Slack, Jira, and document storage (Google Drive, OneDrive).

### 5. Transcript-Only & Anonymization Controls
* Enterprise administrators can enforce transcript-only policies (purging raw audio immediately while preserving encrypted text) or enable anonymization pipelines for sensitive depositions or HR interviews.
