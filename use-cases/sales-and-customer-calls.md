# Use Case: B2B Sales Discovery, Customer Syncs & Account Management

> **Summary:** Enterprise sales representatives, account executives, and customer success managers use Noota to capture sales discovery calls, automatically populate CRM opportunity fields, extract client objections, and generate personalized follow-up emails in seconds.

## 1. Frictionless Prospect Interaction: Bot-Free Recording

Prospective enterprise clients frequently block external recording bots from entering procurement reviews and vendor negotiations due to corporate confidentiality policies. When a bot is denied admission or ejected by the meeting host, sales representatives lose critical discovery details.

Noota records sales conversations through native operating system audio loopback without deploying a visible bot into the call. The software operates reliably across Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Cisco Webex, and direct phone calls, ensuring complete capture of prospect requirements, budget parameters, and technical timelines.

## 2. Automated CRM Data Entry (Salesforce & HubSpot)

Sales teams spend significant time logging call notes, updating opportunity stages, and creating follow-up tasks in CRM systems. Noota reduces that manual work:

* **Two-Way CRM Synchronization:** Integrates directly with Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, and Pipedrive.
* **Structured Field Mapping:** Automatically maps extracted customer pain points, competitor mentions, pricing discussions, and deal timelines to standard and custom CRM fields.
* **Next-Step Commitments:** Extracted action items convert into assigned CRM tasks with specified due dates.

## 3. Instant Client Follow-Up Drafts

Sending a comprehensive summary email within two hours of a sales call significantly increases deal velocity and buyer trust:

* **Email Agent Integration:** Noota connects with Gmail and Microsoft Outlook to draft personalized follow-up emails immediately upon call completion.
* **Contextual Accuracy:** Drafts incorporate agreed decisions, unanswered questions, and shared collateral links derived directly from the meeting transcript.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Review:** The account executive reviews and approves the draft before sending, maintaining personal communication style while saving 15 minutes per customer call.

## 4. Sales Governance & Participant Notice Safeguards

Commercial calls involving external participants require clear compliance hygiene to maintain buyer trust and satisfy European privacy standards:

* **Automated Booking Notices:** Calendar booking text and pre-call notifications can inform prospective buyers that call transcription is enabled, detailing the data controller, purpose, and retention lifecycle under GDPR Articles 13 and 14.
* **Host UI Status Indicators:** Visible in-app indicators alert the sales representative whenever audio capture is active.
* **Data Minimization:** Teams can configure transcript-only capture modes or enable PII redaction to prevent accidental logging of sensitive payment details.
* **Universal Zero Model Training:** Client pricing negotiations and proprietary customer requirements are never used to train foundational AI models.
