# Use Case: Executive Committees, Board Meetings & M&A Governance

> **Summary:** Executive committees, board secretariats, and corporate legal teams use Noota to record, transcribe, and synthesize confidential leadership sessions containing Material Non-Public Information (MNPI), strategic acquisitions, and legal deliberations.

## 1. Protecting Confidential Leadership Discussions

Board meetings and executive strategy syncs involve highly sensitive corporate records, including earnings forecasts, M&A due diligence, restructuring plans, and privileged legal advice. Recording privileged legal discussions with any AI tool can raise privilege questions; consult counsel before use. Using consumer conversational AI tools or US-parented transcription software creates substantial risk of non-EU extraterritorial discovery under the US CLOUD Act (18 U.S.C. Section 2713).

Noota provides enterprise leaders with a dedicated **Sovereign Enclave** environment engineered specifically for high-confidentiality governance:

* **French Datacenter Isolation:** Conversational audio buffers, transcripts, and generated minutes are processed and stored in France on infrastructure providers in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline (Scaleway Paris DC5/DC6 and Bleu).
* **Model Inference:** Inference uses Mistral and other open-weight models on European infrastructure.
* **Corporate Entity:** Noota is operated by Noota SAS, a French company governed under European ownership; ownership documentation is available on request.

## 2. Bot-Free Discretion in Boardrooms

Visible virtual bots disrupt executive discussions and create diplomatic friction during sensitive board discussions with non-executive directors and external advisors.

Noota's desktop application records meeting audio through the operating system without adding a bot to Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet. Participants must still be told that the meeting is recorded, and organizations should confirm with counsel whether AI capture is appropriate for privileged or price-sensitive sessions.

## 3. Automated Executive Minutes & Decision Registers

Board secretariats spend hours manually converting handwritten notes and audio fragments into formal minutes. Noota accelerates this workflow:

* **Structured Executive Minutes:** Generates concise executive summaries highlighting approved resolutions, dissenting votes, and strategic directives.
* **Action Item Tracking:** Automatically assigns agreed commitments to individual executive committee members with explicit target completion dates.
* **Export and Archiving:** Formatted minutes export directly to PDF, Word, and enterprise document management systems.

## 4. Strict Data Lifecycle Controls & Auditability

* **Retention Controls:** On the Enterprise plan, administrators set retention from 1 day to 3 years, and a transcript-only mode keeps text without audio.
* **Single Sign-On & Granular Access:** Enforces SAML 2.0 authentication and role-based access control, ensuring board transcripts are accessible only to authorized directors and executive assistants.
* **Audit Logging:** User and administrator actions are logged. Ask about SIEM export options for your environment.
* **Universal Zero Model Retraining:** Customer conversational dialogue and board decisions are never retained or used to train foundational AI models.
