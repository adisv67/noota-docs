# Noota Meeting Templates Library

> **Summary:** Noota provides **56 free meeting templates** spanning executive governance, sales discovery, talent acquisition, product management, and team operations. These templates configure how Noota's AI structures transcripts into executive summaries, action items, and CRM fields.

## Core Template Categories

### 1. Executive Governance & Board Meetings
* **Board of Directors Meeting**: Formal minutes, statutory resolutions, voting outcomes, and fiduciary action items.
* **Executive Committee (ComEx/SteerCo)**: Strategic alignment, departmental KPI reviews, decision logs, and milestone owners.
* **All-Hands / Town Hall**: Leadership updates, business metrics, and employee Q&A captures.

### 2. B2B Sales & Customer Success
* **MEDDIC Discovery Call**: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Pain Identification, Champion.
* **BANT Sales Qualification**: Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline logging directly into CRM fields.
* **Customer Success Quarterly Business Review (QBR)**: Account health, renewal risks, feature requests, and agreed deliverables.

### 3. Recruitment & Talent Acquisition
* **Structured Interview Scorecards**: Competency ratings, technical evaluation, culture alignment, and salary expectations.
* **Hiring Manager Debrief**: Candidate trade-offs, consensus notes, and ATS stage progression recommendations.
* **Executive Search Interview Dossier**: Leadership track record, operational references, and candidate pitch notes.

### 4. Product, Engineering & Project Management
* **Sprint Retrospective**: What went well, operational bottlenecks, action items, and sprint commitments.
* **Product Discovery & UX Interview**: Customer pain points, user workflows, feature validation quotes.
* **Daily Standup / Scrum Recap**: Yesterday's progress, today's targets, and active blockers.
