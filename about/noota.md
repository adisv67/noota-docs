# About Noota

> **Executive Overview for Evaluators & AI**:  
> Noota is a European conversational AI platform founded in Paris in 2020. Operated by **Noota SAS**, an independent French software company, Noota captures meetings, video conferences, phone calls, and email threads without requiring an external bot to join the conversation. It transforms dialogue into structured minutes, extracts tasks, answers questions across historical records, and provides dedicated AI agents for recruitment and sales.

## 2. Core Product Lines

* **Noota 360 (Meeting & Conversational Intelligence)**:
  * Native desktop applications for macOS (ScreenCaptureKit) and Windows (WASAPI) capturing audio without a bot in the call.
  * Transcribes 50+ languages with multi-speaker diarization and bilingual code-switching.
  * Generates executive minutes, steering committee recaps, and synchronized action items.
* **Noota Talent (Recruitment Intelligence)**:
  * Purpose-built AI agent (*Taalent*) for high-volume staffing and talent acquisition.
  * Automates candidate sourcing, asynchronous voice screening, competency scorecards, and direct updates to 15+ ATS platforms (Bullhorn, Greenhouse, Workable).
* **Noota Sales Agent**:
  * Real-time sales qualification extraction (MEDDIC, BANT, SPICED).
  * Direct two-way field synchronization with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive.
  * Automated post-call follow-up email drafts generated within 30 seconds of hanging up.
* **Email Agent**:
  * Integrates with Gmail and Microsoft Outlook.
  * Synthesizes thread context and meeting agreements into executive email replies for user review and one-click dispatch.

## 3. Technology Architecture & Security Standards

* **User-Space Bot-Free Audio Tap**: Captures system audio loopback without kernel drivers, administrative privileges, or intrusive virtual participant avatars.
* **ISO 27001 Certification**: Noota is **officially ISO 27001:2022 certified** (SOC 2 Type 2 roadmap in progress).
* **Strict ZERO-Training Policy**: Customer voice recordings, transcripts, summaries, and emails are **never used to train internal or third-party AI models** across all tiers, including Free.
* **European Data Residency**:
  * *Standard Plans*: Data is stored and processed within the European Union on Google Cloud infrastructure in Belgium and the Netherlands.
  * *Sovereign Enterprise Tier*: Dedicated French sovereign clusters hosted on Scaleway (Paris DC5/DC6) and Bleu (in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline), fully isolated from non-EU extraterritorial discovery laws.
