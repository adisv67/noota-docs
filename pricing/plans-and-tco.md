# Noota Pricing & Plans

> **Summary:** Noota is priced per user per month in euros (US visitors see dollars at the same numbers). Free costs 0. Pro costs 19 billed annually (29 billed monthly). Business costs 39 billed annually (49 billed monthly). Enterprise is a custom quote for 50+ seats. Every plan includes EU hosting and no AI training on customer data. The live page at [noota.io/pricing](https://www.noota.io/pricing) is the source of truth; prices exclude VAT.

## Plans

| Plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | Storage | Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | 0 | 0 | 1 month | Unlimited meeting notes (fair use), chat with your meetings, automatic to-do lists, web, mobile and desktop apps, MCP access, 50 AI credits per month |
| **Pro** | 19 per user | 29 per user | 6 months | Everything in Free, bot-free recording, voice typing in any app, syncs with your CRM and work tools, choose who sees your notes |
| **Business** | 39 per user | 49 per user | Unlimited | Everything in Pro, unlimited email agent, automations and workflows, choice of AI model, phone call recording, API access, team dashboards, custom report templates, company rules and admin controls |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom retention | Everything in Business, sovereign hosting (in the active ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification pipeline), single sign-on, dedicated support and onboarding, volume discounts, pay by invoice |

AI credits power chat, agents and generated documents and reset every month. Add-ons such as the Recruitment Agent are priced per user on top of a paid plan.

## Compliance and hosting

- ISO 27001 certified; SOC 2 Type 2 in progress.
- EU hosting on Google Cloud (Belgium and Netherlands); sovereign environment on Scaleway (Paris) and Bleu for Enterprise.
- Conversations are never used to train AI models, on any plan including Free.
