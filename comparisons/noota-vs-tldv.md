# Noota vs tl;dv

> **Summary:** tl;dv is built around recorded video, timestamped highlights and sharing clips. Noota is built around structured notes, tasks, email follow-ups and CRM or ATS sync, and records without a bot via a desktop app on macOS and Windows. Noota hosts EU customer data in Belgium and the Netherlands, offers a sovereign environment on Enterprise, never trains on customer data on any plan, and is ISO 27001 certified. tl;dv pricing and plan limits change often and third-party sources disagree, so check tldv.io for current figures.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | tl;dv |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Focus | Notes, tasks, email agent, CRM and ATS sync | Video recording, highlights and clips |
| Bot-free capture | Yes (desktop app) | Check its site for current capture options |
| CRM sync | From Pro (19 per user per month, annual) | Salesforce and HubSpot; plan tier varies by source, check tldv.io |
| Hosting | EU; sovereign option on Enterprise | Check its security page |
| AI training on customer data | Never, on any plan | See its privacy policy |
| Recruitment | Interview scorecards, ATS integrations | General meeting notes |

## Where each fits

- **Noota** fits teams that want text outputs and automation rather than reviewing recordings.
- **tl;dv** fits teams whose main need is sharing video moments with colleagues or customers.
