# Noota vs Jamie

> **Summary:** Both products capture meetings on the user's device without a bot and state ISO 27001 certification. Jamie states that audio is streamed and not stored; Noota keeps audio by default and offers a transcript-only mode. Facts below come from each vendor's public pages on the verification date.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Jamie |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Capture | Desktop app with no bot in the call (macOS and Windows); an optional bot is also available | Runs on the device with no bot; works for online and in-person meetings |
| Audio | Audio is retained by default; a transcript-only mode is available | Audio streamed, never stored |
| Certifications | ISO 27001 certified; SOC 2 Type 2 in progress | ISO 27001 certified; GDPR compliant; aligned with DORA |
| Data hosting | EU hosting (Google Cloud, Belgium and Netherlands); optional French sovereign environment on Enterprise | Data stays in the EU |
| Training on customer data | Customer data is not used to train models, on any plan | Never used to train models |
| Pricing | Free (50 AI credits per month); Pro and Business per user per month billed annually; custom Enterprise (see noota.io/pricing) | Free (EUR 0); Pro EUR 33 (yearly) or EUR 39 per user per month; higher plans list SCIM provisioning |

## Where each fits

- **Jamie** fits individuals and teams that want a stateless approach where audio is not stored, with EU data location and ISO 27001.
- **Noota** fits organizations that want to keep audio for playback, or that need an optional French sovereign environment.
