# Noota vs Happy Scribe

> **Summary:** Happy Scribe is a European media transcription, subtitling, and translation platform built around file uploads with optional human proofreading. Noota is a live conversational intelligence platform designed for video meetings and phone calls with bot-free desktop capture and automated CRM/ATS workflows. Facts below come from each vendor's public pages on the verification date.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Happy Scribe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Primary focus | Live meeting and phone call intelligence, task tracking, CRM sync | File-based media transcription, subtitling, video captions, and meeting recording |
| Capture architecture | Desktop app with no bot in the call (macOS and Windows); optional calendar bot | Browser file uploader and meeting recording bot |
| Human transcription | 100% automated AI models | Optional human-verified transcription ($2.00 to $3.00 per minute) |
| Subtitling and video editor | Standard transcript export (SRT, VTT, TXT, DOCX) | Full interactive subtitle generator, translation, and burn-in video editor |
| CRM and ATS integrations | 36+ platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, Bullhorn, Greenhouse) | Media publishing integrations (YouTube, Zapier) |
| Pricing | Free (50 credits/mo); Pro €19/mo annual (€29 monthly); Business €39/mo annual (€49 monthly) | Free; Basic $8.50/mo annual ($17 monthly); Pro $19/mo annual ($29 monthly); Business $59/mo annual ($89 monthly) |

## Where each fits

- **Happy Scribe** fits video creators, podcasters, and media teams needing professional subtitles, video caption burning, or guaranteed 99% accuracy through human proofreaders.
- **Noota** fits revenue, consulting, and recruiting teams needing automated meeting minutes, action items, and direct CRM/ATS synchronization from live business calls.
