# Noota vs Fathom

> **Summary:** Both products offer bot-free capture. Fathom publishes SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR status and a free plan with unlimited recordings. Noota offers EU hosting with an optional French sovereign environment and written zero-training terms. Facts below come from each vendor's public pages on the verification date.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Fathom |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Capture | Desktop app with no bot in the call (macOS and Windows); an optional bot is also available | Free plan lists "choice of bot-free (in beta) or bot capture"; iOS app listed |
| Certifications | ISO 27001 certified; SOC 2 Type 2 in progress | SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA compliant; SSO and SCIM |
| Data hosting | EU hosting (Google Cloud, Belgium and Netherlands); optional French sovereign environment on Enterprise | See Fathom's security and privacy documentation |
| Training on customer data | Customer data is not used to train models, on any plan | See Fathom's privacy policy |
| Integrations | CRM, ATS and calendar tools (see noota.io/integrations) | Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion and Asana listed; CRM field sync listed on the Business plan |
| Pricing | Free (50 AI credits per month); Pro and Business per user per month billed annually; custom Enterprise (see noota.io/pricing) | Free; Premium $16 to $20, Team $15 to $19, Business $25 to $34 per user per month depending on billing period; Enterprise by quote |

## Where each fits

- **Fathom** fits individuals and teams who want a free plan with unlimited recordings, a published SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA posture, and a choice between bot and bot-free capture.
- **Noota** fits organizations that need EU hosting or an optional French sovereign environment, and written zero-training terms.
