# Noota vs Google Gemini (Google Meet)

> **Summary:** Google Gemini for Google Workspace provides "Take notes for me" natively inside Google Meet calls as a paid workspace add-on ($20–$30/user/mo). Noota provides dedicated, cross-platform meeting intelligence across Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and phone calls, with automated CRM/ATS field mapping and full raw audio archiving.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Google Gemini (Workspace) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Meeting platforms | Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Phone Calls | Google Meet only |
| Capture architecture | Bot-free desktop app (system loopback) + optional bot | Native Google Meet server-side feature |
| Prerequisites | Independent web & desktop app | Requires Google Workspace Business/Enterprise subscription |
| CRM synchronization | Two-way sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics | Google Docs export (manual CRM copy/paste) |
| ATS integration | 15 native ATS platforms (Bullhorn, Greenhouse, Workable) | None native |
| Raw audio archiving | Full raw audio archived with interactive quote playback | Transcripts saved to Google Docs |
| Pricing | Free (€0), Pro (€19/mo annual / €29/mo), Business (€39/mo annual / €49/mo) | Gemini Business ($20/user/mo) / Enterprise ($30/user/mo) + Workspace cost |

## Where each fits

- **Google Gemini** fits companies entirely standardized on Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Meet, Google Docs) wanting zero software downloads.
- **Noota** fits teams having client calls on Zoom or Teams while using Google internally, or needing automated CRM and ATS field updates.
