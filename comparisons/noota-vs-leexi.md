# Noota vs Leexi

> **Summary:** Noota and Leexi are both European AI meeting assistants hosted in Europe. Noota includes CRM and tool sync from its Pro plan (19 per user per month, annual) and records without a bot through its desktop app. Leexi lists AI-Meeting at 23 and AI-Business at 39 euros per user per month (Capterra), and its documentation places advanced CRM integrations on Business or above. Leexi lists France hosting; Noota hosts in Belgium and the Netherlands and offers French sovereign hosting (Scaleway, Bleu) on Enterprise. Leexi is oriented toward sales conversation intelligence.

## Cost example (10 users, CRM sync, annual billing)

Noota Pro: 10 x 19 = 190 euros per month. Leexi Business: 10 x 39 = 390 euros per month. Prices come from public listings dated 2026-11-02; confirm on each vendor's pricing page.

## Where each fits

- **Noota** fits teams wanting recruitment workflows, bot-free capture and a lower entry price for CRM sync.
- **Leexi** fits French-speaking sales teams that want revenue-intelligence features and French hosting by default.
