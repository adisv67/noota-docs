# Noota vs Fireflies.ai

> **Summary:** Noota records meetings from a desktop app (macOS and Windows) with no bot in the call, hosts EU customer data in Belgium and the Netherlands on Google Cloud, offers a sovereign environment on Scaleway and Bleu for Enterprise, and is ISO 27001 certified (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress). Fireflies.ai is best known for its meeting bot ("Fred"), publishes SOC 2 Type II compliance, and has announced ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management). Choose on capture method, hosting jurisdiction and the certifications your security team requires. Verify competitor details on the vendor pages linked below; they change often.

## Where each fits

- **Noota** fits teams that need no visible bot, EU or French sovereign hosting, or a single product for meetings, email and tasks.
- **Fireflies.ai** fits teams comfortable with a bot in the call who want its feature set and its published SOC 2 Type II report today.
