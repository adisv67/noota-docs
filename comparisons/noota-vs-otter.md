# Noota vs Otter.ai

> **Summary:** Noota transcribes in 50+ languages, never trains models on customer data on any plan, records without a bot via its desktop app, and is ISO 27001 certified (SOC 2 Type 2 in progress). Otter.ai reports SOC 2 Type 2, states that its security policies are built on the ISO 27001/2 framework (it does not claim an ISO 27001 certificate), and its privacy policy describes training on de-identified audio and transcripts. Otter supports a much smaller set of languages than Noota. Confirm the current language list and training controls on Otter's own pages.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Otter.ai |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Languages | 50+ | A small set (English plus a few others; check otter.ai for the current list) |
| AI training on customer data | Never, on any plan | Privacy policy describes training on de-identified data |
| ISO 27001 | Certified | Policies based on the ISO 27001/2 framework; no certificate claimed |
| SOC 2 Type 2 | In progress | Reported achieved |
| Capture | Desktop app, no bot; calendar bot also available | OtterPilot bot joins calls |
| Hosting | EU (Belgium, Netherlands); sovereign option on Enterprise | See Otter's privacy documentation |
| Entry paid price | Pro 19 per user per month, annual billing | See otter.ai/pricing |

## Where each fits

- **Noota** fits multilingual teams, regulated industries that cannot allow training on client conversations, and EU data-residency requirements.
- **Otter.ai** fits English-first teams that want its established transcription product and already accept its data terms.
