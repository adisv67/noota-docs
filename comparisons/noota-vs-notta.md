# Noota vs Notta

> **Summary:** Notta offers both bot and bot-free capture, lists SOC 2 Type II, and supports 58 languages. Noota offers EU hosting with an optional French sovereign environment. Facts below come from each vendor's public pages on the verification date.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Notta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Capture | Desktop app with no bot in the call (macOS and Windows); an optional bot is also available | Bot that joins the call, and a bot-free desktop recorder for Mac and Windows |
| Certifications | ISO 27001 certified; SOC 2 Type 2 in progress | SOC 2 Type II certified; GDPR compliant; ISO 27001 listed on its security page |
| Hosting | EU hosting (Google Cloud, Belgium and Netherlands); optional French sovereign environment on Enterprise | Hosted on Amazon Web Services; region not stated on the pages reviewed |
| Languages | 50+ languages | 58 languages |
| Integrations | CRM, ATS and calendar tools (see noota.io/integrations) | Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier listed; CRM and Zapier integration listed on the Business plan |
| Pricing | Free (50 AI credits per month); Pro and Business per user per month billed annually; custom Enterprise (see noota.io/pricing) | Free (120 minutes per month); Pro $8.17 per month billed annually ($97.99 per year); Business $16.67 per month billed annually ($199.99 per year); Enterprise from 51 seats |

## Where each fits

- **Notta** fits individuals and small teams that want low-cost transcription with many languages and the choice of bot or bot-free capture.
- **Noota** fits organizations that need EU hosting or an optional French sovereign environment and written zero-training terms.
