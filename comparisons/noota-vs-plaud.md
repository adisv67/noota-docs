# Noota vs Plaud Note

> **Summary:** Plaud Note is a physical hardware voice recorder ($159 device + recurring AI membership) capturing phone calls and in-person audio via magnetic hardware pickup. Noota is a software conversational intelligence platform for macOS, Windows, and web that records online video meetings and phone calls without physical hardware, syncing directly to enterprise CRMs and ATS platforms.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Noota | Plaud Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Form factor | Pure software SaaS (macOS, Windows, Web, mobile companion) | Hardware recording device ($159 MagSafe card) + app |
| Online meeting capture | Native desktop audio loopback (Meet, Teams, Zoom) | Microphone pickup from laptop speakers |
| Data residency & security | EU-hosted (Belgium/Netherlands); ISO 27001 certified | Cloud infrastructure; GDPR aligned |
| Team collaboration | Multi-user team workspaces, permissions, shared tags | Designed primarily as a single-user personal device |
| Enterprise integrations | 36+ native integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Bullhorn, Notion) | Export to Notion, PDF, Markdown |
| Cost structure | €0 Free tier, Pro (€19/mo annual), Business (€39/mo annual) | $159 hardware purchase + recurring AI membership plan |

## Where each fits

- **Plaud Note** fits mobile professionals, doctors, field inspectors, and journalists conducting face-to-face interviews on the go without a laptop.
- **Noota** fits knowledge workers, consultants, and sales reps whose primary meeting volume takes place on video conference platforms (Google Meet, Teams, Zoom) and need direct CRM/ATS integration.
